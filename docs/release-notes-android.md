The Friends Android beta: plan things to do with your friend groups.

**Installing**
1. Download the `.apk` below on your Android phone.
2. Open it. Android asks to allow installing apps from this source (your browser or file manager). Allow it for this install.
3. Open Friends and sign in, or open the invite link a friend sent you and create your account.

Updates come the same way: install the newer `.apk` over the old one, and your data stays.

**What's in it**
- Groups and invite links.
- A shared backlog of ideas, with custom fields per category (e.g. IMDb ratings for movies).
- The "What should we do?" wheel.
- Polls inside activities.
- A shared calendar with recurring plans and birthdays.

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on this repository, or tell the group admin.
